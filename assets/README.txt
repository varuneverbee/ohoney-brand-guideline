OHONEY BRAND LOGOS — approved files only
Live guide: https://ohoney-brand-guideline.vercel.app

Pick the variant that matches the background. The upright "O" is warm brown
(#20170d) on light grounds and warm cream (#fdfaf3) on dark — never the wrong
pairing, and never neutral black or white.

THREE RULES THAT MATTER
  1. Match the variant to the ground.
  2. Below 64px wide, drop the wordmark and use the icon. The endorsed
     "by EverBee" lockups need 140px — their endorsement row closes up first.
  3. Clear space on all sides = the height of the "O" (1x preferred, 0.5x min).

Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects to, or rebuild these by hand.
The gold mark rgb(208,155,13) is retired — do not use it.
